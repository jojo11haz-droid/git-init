# Between — voiceover scripts (record in your own voice)

One script per video, in English and French. Each line has the moment it
plays in the video and roughly how long you have. Speak calmly, a little
slower than normal conversation, and smile slightly: it shows in the voice.

## How to record (10 minutes, with your phone)

1. **Room:** the quietest room you have, with soft things around (a bedroom or
   a closet full of clothes is perfect; avoid kitchens and bathrooms, they echo).
   Turn off fans, fridge hum, notifications.
2. **Phone:** the built-in Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder (Android) app.
   Hold it about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side
   so your breath doesn't hit it.
3. **Do one line at a time.** Say the line number first ("Line 3"), pause one
   second, say the line, pause two seconds. If you stumble, just say the line
   again: I'll keep the best take.
4. **Read every line twice** in a row, so I can pick the better one.
5. **French and English separately:** one recording per language.
6. **Send me the files:** attach them here, or upload them on GitHub to the
   folder `marketing/voice/` on the branch `claude/between-clinician-auth-vvl9zb`
   (Add file → Upload files).

I'll cut the takes, clean the sound (remove hum, even out the volume), place
each line on its scene, and add soft music underneath.
