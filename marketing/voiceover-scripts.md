# Between — voiceover scripts (record in your own voice)

Two scripts cover all 11 professions, each in English and French: **4
recordings in total**. The on-screen captions stay specific to each
profession (SOAP / DAP / ADIME note, patients / clients / players /
students); the voice stays general so it fits every version. Scene timing is
adjusted to your voice, so speak at your natural pace.

| Script | Used for |
|---|---|
| **A · Body** | physiotherapy, kinesiology, osteopathy, occupational therapy, sports, fitness |
| **B · Mind** | psychotherapy, neuropsychology, recovery, nutrition, schools |

## How to record (about 15 minutes for all four)

1. **Room:** the quietest room you have, with soft things around (a bedroom or
   a closet full of clothes is perfect; avoid kitchens and bathrooms, they echo).
   Turn off fans and notifications.
2. **Phone:** Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder (Android), held a hand's width
   (15–20 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side.
3. **One line at a time:** say the line number ("Line 3"), pause one second,
   say the line, pause two seconds. Stumbled? Just say it again.
4. **Read every line twice** in a row so I can keep the better take.
5. **One recording per script and language** (A-English, A-French,
   B-English, B-French).
6. **Send the files:** attach them here, or upload them on GitHub to
   `marketing/voice/` on the branch `claude/between-clinician-auth-vvl9zb`
   (Add file → Upload files).

Speak calmly, a little slower than in conversation, and smile slightly: it
shows in the voice. I'll clean the sound, place each line on its scene and add
soft music underneath.
