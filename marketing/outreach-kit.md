# Between — clinic outreach kit (EN / FR)

Everything to copy and paste when reaching professionals: cold emails with
follow-ups, front-desk and phone scripts, LinkedIn messages, and the lines to
swap per profession. French versions follow each English one.

Replace anything in [brackets]. Keep emails short: the shorter, the more replies.

---

## 0. How to use this

**Who to write to first (in this order)**
1. **Solo practitioners** — owner, user and buyer in one person, no gatekeeper.
   Find them in the public directories of the professional orders (OPPQ,
   OPQ, FKQ, OEQ, ODQ…), on Google Maps (small practices with one name), and on
   Instagram/LinkedIn.
2. **Clinicians inside clinics** (an employed physio, psychotherapist…), not
   the owner. One clinician who says "I tried it with three patients and it
   saved me time" gets software into a clinic.
3. **The owner / decider** — only once you have their name (ask the front
   desk "who chooses your software?") or a clinician has tried it.

**How the emails work now**
- **Email 1 has no link.** It ends with an easy question ("Would a 1-minute
  video be useful?"). Getting a reply is the goal: replies tell Gmail and
  Outlook you're not spam, and you learn who's interested.
- **When they say yes**, send the "When they reply" email with the video link
  and the founding offer.
- **No reply?** Follow-up 1 (4 days later, same thread) gives the link anyway.
  Follow-up 2 (5 days after that) closes politely. Then stop. Most replies
  come from the follow-ups.

**Before you start sending (once)**
1. Send from **owner@betweenpsych.com**, never a Gmail address.
2. Go to **mail-tester.com**, send one email to the address it shows, and check
   the score. Below 9/10? Its report says what to fix (usually the SPF, DKIM and
   DMARC records at your domain provider; your email provider's help pages walk
   you through them).
3. **Send by hand, 10–15 a day**, from your normal mailbox. No mass-mailing
   tools, no BCC lists: a new domain that sends 100 emails on day one goes to spam.

**Rules that matter**
- Write to a **person by name**, not info@ (the front desk filters it). Find a
  clinician on the clinic's "Our team" page.
- First line about *them* (their clinic, a post, their specialty). One line is enough.
- Plain text: no images, no attachments, no bold.
- One question they can answer yes/no. Never "can we book a meeting?".
- Send Tuesday–Thursday, 7–9 a.m., before their first patients.
- **Combine channels:** after email 1, drop a flyer at the front desk ("I sent
  Dr. [name] an email about this"); after follow-up 1, a quick call (section 5).

**Founding clinic offer (until January 31, first 10 clinics):** 60 days free
instead of 14, price locked in. It's automatic on the site while spots remain.
It goes in the "When they reply" email and in follow-up 1, not in email 1.

**Track what works.** Every link below carries `?ref=`: the site counts visits
by source (owner dashboard → site stats). Use `ref=email`, `ref=flyer`,
`ref=linkedin`, `ref=phone`. Change it if you want to compare two versions
(`ref=email-a`, `ref=email-b`).

**Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) — required in every cold email.** You may email
a business address that is published publicly (website, directory) if the
message is about their work. Every message must show **who you are, a mailing
address, and an easy way to opt out**, and you must honour opt-outs within 10
business days. The signature below covers this; keep it on every email and
keep a list of people who asked not to be contacted.

## 4. Front desk (in person) — 30 seconds

Bring the flyer (`marketing/flyers/`). Don't ask for the owner.

**EN**
> "Hi! I'll be quick, I know you're busy. I make a tool that lets patients check in between visits, so the physio knows how they've been before they come in. Could you pass this sheet to whoever chooses your software? The QR code shows a one-minute video."
>
> *(If they're open)* "Who would that usually be? I'll write to them directly so I don't take your time."
>
> *(Leaving)* "Thanks so much. Have a good day!"

**FR**
> « Bonjour! Je fais vite, je sais que vous êtes occupé(e). Je fais un outil qui permet aux patients de faire un suivi entre les visites, pour que le physio sache comment ils vont avant qu'ils arrivent. Pourriez-vous remettre cette feuille à la personne qui choisit vos logiciels? Le code QR montre une vidéo d'une minute. »
>
> *(Si la personne est ouverte)* « Ce serait qui, d'habitude? Je lui écrirai directement pour ne pas vous prendre de temps. »
>
> *(En partant)* « Merci beaucoup, bonne journée! »

Write the date, clinic and the name you got in a list; email that person the
same day with section 3 ("[Front-desk name] suggested I write to you").
