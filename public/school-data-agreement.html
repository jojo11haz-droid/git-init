School Data Agreement

Template version: August 21, 2026
Template — not legal advice. This is a starting point so a school and Between can agree in writing on how student data is handled. Student check-ins can include health-related information about minors, so this should be reviewed and finalized by a lawyer, and checked against Quebec's Law 25 and any privacy or education laws that apply to the school, before it is signed.

This School Data Agreement (the "Agreement") is between School legal name, at School address (the "School"), and Between, operated by Jack Hazan (the "Provider"). It takes effect on effective date and covers the School's use of Between for student check-ins.

1. Roles

The School decides why and how student information is used and is the party responsible for it. The Provider processes that information only on the School's behalf and only to run the service described below. The Provider does not use student information for its own purposes and does not sell it.

2. What Between does

Between lets a student send a short voice or text check-in between classes or meetings. Where a student turns it on, Between produces a short summary and simple mood and theme trends that the assigned staff member can read before the next class or meeting. It is a between-session check-in tool. It is not a health record, an assessment, or a crisis service.

3. Information processed

  • Staff accounts: name, email, and password (stored hashed).
  • Student accounts: name and email.
  • Check-in content: the text and voice notes students submit, mood ratings, and theme tags. This may include sensitive health-related information.
  • AI-generated content: only where a student opts in, summaries and theme tags derived from their check-ins.

4. Consent and minors

Many students are minors. The School confirms that, before inviting a student who is under 18, it has obtained any parental or guardian consent required for that student to use Between. At sign-up, each person also confirms in the product that they are 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian has agreed to their use. AI analysis is off by default and runs only after the student gives explicit, opt-in consent, which they can withdraw at any time.

5. Who can see student data

  • The assigned staff member: a student's check-ins, and any opt-in summaries, are visible only to the staff member they were invited by. Data is scoped per staff account and is not shared across the School by default.
  • The student: students see and control their own data and can request correction or deletion.
  • The Provider: the Provider accesses student data only as needed to operate, support, and secure the service.

6. Sub-processors and where data is stored

The Provider uses a small number of service providers to run Between, and student information — including check-in content — may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries where these providers operate:

  • Render — application and database hosting.
  • Stripe — subscription billing (no student check-in content).
  • Email and voice transcription providers, where configured, to deliver messages and transcribe voice check-ins.

Because this can involve transferring personal information outside Quebec, the parties handle it with Quebec's Law 25 in mind. The Provider will keep the School informed of the sub-processors it relies on.

7. Security

The Provider protects information with, at minimum: encryption in transit (HTTPS with HSTS), hashed staff and student passwords, optional multi-factor authentication for staff, per-account access scoping, expiring signed links for voice files, and rate limiting on sign-in and sign-up. These measures may improve over time but will not be materially weakened while this Agreement is in effect.

8. Retention and deletion

Student data is kept only while it is needed to provide the service. A staff member can delete a student, which removes that student's check-ins, summaries, voice notes, and alerts. On request from the School, or when this Agreement ends, the Provider will delete or return the School's student data within a reasonable period, except where it must keep something to meet a legal obligation.

9. If something goes wrong

If the Provider becomes aware of a security incident affecting the School's student data, it will notify the School without undue delay, share what it knows, and cooperate on the School's response.

10. Not a crisis service

Between is not a crisis or emergency service and does not monitor check-ins in real time. Crisis resources are shown to students directly if risk language is detected, but the School remains responsible for its own duty-of-care and safeguarding procedures.

11. Term, changes, and governing law

This Agreement runs while the School uses Between and can be ended by either party in writing. Material changes to this Agreement will be shared with the School in advance. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Quebec and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
