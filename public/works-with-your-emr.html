Works with your practice software

Keep Jane, Clinicmaster or whatever you chart in. Between sits beside it.

Between is not a new system to switch to. It covers the time between appointments, and what it produces goes into the chart you already keep. Nothing to install, no IT project.

1. Bring your client list in

  1. In your practice software, export your client or patient list as a spreadsheet file (CSV).
  2. In Between, open your dashboard, choose the button that adds people from a photo or list, and pick that file.
  3. Check the first names, untick anyone you do not want, and add them. Each person gets a private invite link.

The file is read on your own device and is never uploaded. Only the first names you keep are sent to Between. Emails, phone numbers, birth dates and everything else in the file stay with you.

2. Put the note in the chart

  1. Before a visit, Between has a short summary and the trend ready. After it, dictate a few words and Between drafts the chart note in SOAP, DAP or ADIME.
  2. Read it over and correct it. It is your note.
  3. Choose Copy to chart and paste it into the chart entry, or Save as PDF and attach the file to the chart, the same way you add any note or document.
