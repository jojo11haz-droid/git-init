Why Between exists

In psychology, in sports and in nutrition, I kept seeing the same thing: the real progress happens between appointments. The hard night, the skipped meal, the sore knee, the week that finally clicked. Most of it never reaches the person helping, or it arrives a week later as a fuzzy memory.

I wanted something that helps on both sides. Clients get an easy way to say how they're really doing, the moment it matters, by voice or text, in under a minute. Professionals get that time back: a short summary and the trend before the next visit, a drafted chart note, and more of each session spent on the person in front of them.

If it gives you back some time and helps someone feel heard between visits, it's doing its job.

Jack

How Between treats your data

Health information deserves care. These are the rules Between is built on:

  • Consent first. AI summaries stay off until the client turns them on, and they can turn them off anytime.
  • Each professional sees only their own clients. Nothing is shared across a clinic.
  • Everyone can export or delete their data whenever they want.
  • Built with Canadian privacy law in mind, including Quebec's Law 25. Professionals in regulated fields are licence-verified before their dashboard opens.
  • Between is not a crisis service. Clients always see crisis resources directly, and never wait on anyone.
