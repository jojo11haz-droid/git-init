Privacy & security, in plain words

The questions clinics ask before they invite their first client, with straight answers. The legal versions are linked at the bottom.

  • AI is off until the client turns it on
  • Each professional sees only their own clients
  • Never sold, never used to train AI
  • Built with Law 25 and PIPEDA in mind

Where is the data stored?

In the United States. The application runs on Render and the database is hosted by Neon. Because that is outside Quebec and Canada, we handle it with Quebec's Law 25 and the federal PIPEDA in mind. Every provider, what it does and where it processes data is listed on the subprocessors page.

Who can see a client's check-ins?

Only the professional who invited that client. Data is kept separate for each professional's account and is not shared across a clinic. The client sees and controls their own data. Between accesses it only when needed to run, support or secure the service.

How does consent work?

The client creates their own account from your invite. AI summaries are off by default: the client turns them on, and can turn them off at any time. Check-ins work fully without AI. Recording an appointment to draft your note only starts after you confirm the client agreed. You remain responsible for any consent your professional order requires.

What does the AI see? Is it used for training?

Only check-ins from clients who turned AI on, plus documents or session transcripts you ask it to write up. It is processed by Anthropic, and under our agreement it is not used to train their models. Summaries are a neutral read for you, never a diagnosis: your judgment always decides.

Are session recordings kept?

No. When you record an appointment or dictate notes, the audio is transcribed, the note is drafted, and then the audio and transcript are dropped. Only the note you choose to keep in your own records remains.

How are accounts protected?

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS with HSTS). Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Professionals can turn on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. Voice and photo files open only through links that expire, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

Who can sign up as a professional?

Professionals in regulated fields give their licence or order number, and it is checked before their dashboard opens.

Can data be exported or deleted?

Yes. Clients can export or delete their own history at any time. When you remove a client, you get a short window to download their record, then their check-ins, summaries, voice notes and photos are permanently deleted. When a clinic leaves, we delete or return its data on request.

What if something goes wrong?

If a security incident affects your clients' data, we tell you without undue delay, share what we know, and help with any notice you must give under the law.

Am I on call through Between?

No. Between is not a crisis service and nobody monitors check-ins in real time. A summary waits for your next appointment. If a client writes something that suggests risk, they see crisis resources right away, without waiting on anyone.
