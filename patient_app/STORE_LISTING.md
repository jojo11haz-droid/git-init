### Full description
Between is a quiet place to check in on how you're really doing — on your own,
or with a professional who's helping you.

A check-in takes about ten seconds. Write a few words, record a voice memo, or
answer a couple of guided questions — the moment something comes up. No forms,
no appointment, no one waiting on the other end.

Over time, your mood and the themes behind your harder days take shape, so a
rough stretch — or real progress — is something you can actually see. If you're
recovering from an injury, you can map where it hurts and how it feels, and
watch the pain ease week by week.

WHAT YOU GET
• Check in in seconds, by voice or text
• Your own mood and theme trends over time
• A body map for pain and recovery
• Gentle, optional reminders on your own schedule
• Crisis resources always one tap away

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your entries are yours. AI analysis is opt-in and off by default. You can
export or delete your data anytime. Built with Canadian privacy law in mind —
PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25.

ON YOUR OWN, OR WITH A PRO
Use Between as a private journal, or accept an invite from your therapist,
physiotherapist or other professional — who then sees a short summary before
your next session, only with your consent.

PREMIUM
Start free. Premium (available at betweenpsych.com) adds unlimited check-ins,
guided journaling packs, a weekly reflection, photo attachments, and the full
advanced-stats view.

Between is not a crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or thinking
about suicide, call or text 988, or call 911.

## Store privacy notes (for the data-safety / privacy questionnaire)
- Data collected: account email, check-in text/voice, mood/pain ratings,
  optional photos, optional health metrics (with permission).
- Purpose: app functionality (storing and summarizing the user's own
  check-ins). AI summaries are opt-in.
- Sharing: with the user's own clinician only, and only if the user consents.
  No advertising, no data sale.
- Security: data in transit over HTTPS; account is password-protected.
- Deletion: users can request erasure in-app (Settings → erase data).
- Data location: third-party cloud (currently Render), may be in the US;
  cross-border transfers handled with Quebec's Law 25 in mind.
